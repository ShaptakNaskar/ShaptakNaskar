## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ShaptakNaskar/hanamimi/plus/assets/icon/icon.png" width="30" align="top" alt="" /> Hanamimi 花耳

**A kawaii, offline-first music player with a beagle mascot that bops to your music.** Named after a real dog.

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ShaptakNaskar/hanamimi/plus/docs/screenshots/home.png" width="158" alt="Home" />
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ShaptakNaskar/hanamimi/plus/docs/screenshots/now-playing.png" width="158" alt="Now Playing" />
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ShaptakNaskar/hanamimi/plus/docs/screenshots/lyrics-karaoke.png" width="158" alt="Karaoke lyrics" />
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ShaptakNaskar/hanamimi/plus/docs/screenshots/adaptive.png" width="158" alt="Adaptive theme" />
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ShaptakNaskar/hanamimi/plus/docs/screenshots/stats.png" width="158" alt="Listening stats" />
</div>

- 🎤 **Word-by-word karaoke lyrics** — embedded tags → Musixmatch richsync → LRCLIB, with per-track sync nudging
- 🧠 **Recommendations computed entirely on-device** — recency-weighted plays, a co-play Markov matrix, a skip signal, and audio-feature similarity extracted for free during the visualizer's FFT decode
- 🔒 **A three-position privacy dial** — local-only by default, anonymous discovery, or full YT Music sign-in. You pick how much leaves the phone
- 🌈 Mood themes, real FFT visualizer, true two-player crossfade, folder browsing, sleep timer
- 🖥️ **Native Linux & Windows builds** (media_kit/libmpv, no Electron) with MPRIS/SMTC — plus `oneko`, the classic cursor-chasing cat, living in your window

## 🌧️ SappyWeather

**A weather app that changes its entire personality depending on your mood.** Five themes, each with its own splash screen, weather-reactive gradients and card styling — from a neon cyberpunk HUD with particle rain and scanlines, to soft pastel night.

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ShaptakNaskar/WeatherMan/master/Screenshot.png" width="720" alt="SappyWeather" />
</div>

- 📡 Real-time data via **Open-Meteo** — no API key, no account, no tracking
- 📊 24-hour timeline, 10-day range bars, **AQI with full pollutant breakdown**, minute-by-minute rain outlook, sunrise/sunset arc
- 🧥 "What to wear" advice derived from actual conditions, not vibes
- 🔔 Smart notifications — morning briefing, evening outlook, severe weather alerts, trend insights, all on background sync
- 🥚 Tap the cloud icon seven times for the weather-FX debug simulator

## 📱 NothingMuch — keeping a ROM alive

When **arter97** stopped maintaining the NothingMuch ROM for the Nothing Phone (2), I picked it up. I now maintain a best-effort continuation of the ROM plus a **custom `sm8475` kernel with SUSFS integrated** — real builds, flashed on my own daily driver, for people who'd otherwise be stuck.
